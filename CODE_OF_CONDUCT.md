# Community Code of Conduct

CHG welcomes technical disagreement. It does not accept harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets, or personal attacks.

Participants must:

- criticize evidence, designs, and code rather than people;
- disclose conflicts of interest relevant to a proposal or certification;
- respect coordinated vulnerability disclosure;
- avoid misleading claims of Cogiens affiliation or certification;
- preserve the provenance and license of contributed work.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove abusive or dangerous content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or users. Enforcement decisions should be documented privately and reviewed by at least two maintainers whenever practical.
