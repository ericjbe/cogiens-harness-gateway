# Developer Certificate of Origin

By adding `Signed-off-by` to a commit, the contributor certifies that at least one of the following is true:

1. The contribution was created by the contributor and the contributor may submit it under the project's license.
2. The contribution is based on prior work that, to the contributor's knowledge, is covered by an appropriate open-source license and the contributor may submit modifications under the same license.
3. The contribution was provided by another person who certified one of these conditions, and the contributor has not knowingly modified its provenance.
4. The contribution and sign-off may be maintained publicly and redistributed as part of the project.

This repository follows the Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1 process. The canonical text is maintained at https://developercertificate.org/.
